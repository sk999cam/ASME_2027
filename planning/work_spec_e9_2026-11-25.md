## W1 Regional closure split against the 193M b010 (figures F15, F16)

For each of RANS 5M, RANS 50M, 20M LES, 50M and 100M against the 193M b010:
- Regions: the point-wise region set of `regions_pp` (C19): metering, diffuser, exit, approach layer, jet
  (x/D 0–3), near film (3–10), far film (10–25).
- Use the same thresholds as now: |S*| > 0.05 U/D, |∇T| > 0.05 ΔT0/D, counter-gradient points excluded.
- Use one common point set per pair, so that the terms add up exactly.
- Quantities: volume averages of
  - ln(μ_eff ratio), split into the density, energy (½ ln k), length (ln L_eff) and alignment (ln cos θ) terms;
  - ln(α_eff ratio), split into density, energy, **thermal mixing length ln ℓ_T and correlation ln ρ_uT as two
    separate terms**, and alignment ln cos φ;
  - ln(Pr_t,eff ratio), split into the length ratio and the alignment ratio;
  - the 193M b010 regional Pr_t,eff itself (volume average of the ln, and its exp).
- Errors: delete-one jackknife SE over the reference batches. Also report the volume fraction used per region.
- **Change, 2026-11-25 (owner decision, film/jet split):** also report per region the two terms of
  ln(α_eff/α_eff^193) = ln(μ_eff/μ_eff^193) + ln(Pr_t,eff^193/Pr_t,eff), with the regional values from the
  gradient-weighted least-squares fit, no point thresholds: μ_R = Σ(−a:S*)/Σ2|S*|²,
  α_R = Σ(−q·∇T)/Σ|∇T|², Pr_R = c_p μ_R/α_R, plus the fit qualities R²_q = 1 − Σ|q + α_R∇T|²/Σ|q|² and the
  same for the stress. The ℓ_T / ρ_uT sub-split is now optional (one number per region).
- Output: text tables in the form of the existing `results_pp/regions_se.txt`, `regions_alpha_se.txt` and
  `regions_prt_se.txt`, one folder per pair. Proposed names: `analysis/meff_<rung>_c193b10/results_pp/`.
- Also, if not already there, the 50M- and 100M-against-193M pairs, redone at b010 on the point-wise regions.
